About me

A teacher first, and a teacher of teachers.

I studied chemistry at the American University of Beirut, and business, and I tutor across all three major curricula: IB, British and American. I teach most subjects, including chemistry and the other sciences, maths, business courses and university science courses, as well as pre-med classes and SAT Maths. I also run university counselling sessions.

Today I'm Assistant Head Teacher at a Dubai IB school, where I lead teaching and learning and coach other teachers. That work shapes my tutoring. I know what strong exam answers look like, how mark schemes reward clear reasoning, and how to spot the exact step where a student gets lost.

Sessions are calm, structured and honest. You'll always leave knowing what you improved and what to practise next.
